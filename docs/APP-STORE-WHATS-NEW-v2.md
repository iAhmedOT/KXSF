# KXSF 2.0

Version 2 brings more of the station into the app:

- Live radio with a refreshed player and current-show information.
- The official weekly schedule and host information where available.
- Recent KXSF Live videos from the station's YouTube channel.
- A Now Playing Home Screen widget and Live Activity support.
- Station information, social links, and ways to support KXSF.

Read [the case study](https://iahmedot.com/case-studies/kxsf/) for the story behind the update.

Thanks for listening to KXSF 102.5 FM, San Francisco Community Radio.
