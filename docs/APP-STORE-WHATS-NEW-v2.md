# KXSF 2.0 — App Store “What’s New”

## Recommended customer-facing copy

A new KXSF listening experience is here.

- Listen live with a refreshed player and current show information.
- Browse KXSF’s official show schedule and discover the hosts behind the programs.
- Watch the latest KXSF Live videos from the station’s official YouTube channel.
- Keep KXSF close at hand with a Now Playing Home Screen widget and Live Activity support.
- Enjoy a cleaner, more reliable app built for today’s iPhone.

Thanks for listening to KXSF 102.5 FM — San Francisco Community Radio.

## Short alternative

KXSF has been rebuilt for a better way to listen. Stream live, explore the official show schedule, watch KXSF Live videos, and keep up with Now Playing through the Home Screen widget and Live Activity support.

## Metadata notes (not for customers)

- Version: `2.0`
- Minimum OS: iOS 18.0
- “Live Activity support” is intentionally phrased as support rather than a promise that one is always visible; it appears when live playback is active and iOS permits it.
- The copy names only verified listener-facing functionality from the V2 release: live playback, official schedule/host data where supplied, official KXSF YouTube content, Home Screen widget, and Live Activities.
